1. EXECUTABLE OPERATING ENVELOPE

For the synthetic spinach case, 8 degrees Celsius is the author-declared temperature
ceiling used by the operating-envelope check and by the mode-neutral temperature-
violation outcome. The MCP envelope also uses 85 to 95 percent relative humidity. These
are benchmark values, not universal storage, FSMA, HACCP, release, or diversion rules.

The separate outcome quality flag is true when remaining modeled quality 1-rho is below
0.50. The benchmark does not classify minor, moderate, or critical excursions from
duration or degree-hours, and it does not use a time-temperature-integration release
threshold.

2. EXECUTABLE SPOILAGE-RISK MODEL

All modes use the same synthetic mechanistic trajectory:

  dC/dt = -k(T,H)*alpha(t)*C; rho = 1-C
  k(T,H) = 0.0021*exp[8000*(1/277.15 - 1/(T_C+273.15))]
           *(1 + 0.25*RH/100) per hour
  alpha(t) = t/(t+12).

The midpoint update keeps rho in [0,1] and monotonically non-decreasing. The values were
not fitted to observed spinach quality labels, so rho is modeled risk rather than a
microbial count, shelf-life validation, or safety determination.

3. EXCLUDED CLAIMS

The benchmark does not create HACCP deviation records, quarantine or release product,
notify a food-safety officer, mandate sensor cadence, or impose record retention. Those
operational decisions require validated product evidence and applicable local rules.
