At nominal intensity, the heatwave perturbation begins at simulation hour 24. From hour
24 through hour 48 it adds

  temperature: 20*(1-exp(-0.5*(h-24))) degrees Celsius
  relative humidity: 10*(1-exp(-0.5*(h-24))) percentage points.

After hour 48, both additions decay as their peak coefficient multiplied by
exp(-0.1*(h-48)); relative humidity is clipped to [0,100]. The mechanistic spoilage and
volatility columns are then recomputed from the perturbed state for every paired mode.

The scenario does not activate from an observed 35-degree threshold, query a weather
feed, shorten the 120-km cold_chain route, mandate redistribution, alter inspection or
sensor cadence, or notify personnel. Route choice remains probabilistic under the active
policy components. These equations are synthetic stress-test assumptions, not a
validated representation of a specific heatwave or refrigeration system.
