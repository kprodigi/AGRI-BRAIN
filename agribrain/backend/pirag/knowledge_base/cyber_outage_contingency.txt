1. EXECUTABLE SCENARIO PERTURBATION

At the nominal intensity, the cyber_outage scenario starts at simulation hour 24. For
h >= 24, demand is multiplied by 0.15 and temperature receives the additive excursion

  10*(1 - exp(-0.2*(h-24))) degrees Celsius.

Inventory is not directly overwritten by this scenario transform. The common scenario
state is recomputed and supplied to every paired experimental mode.

2. EXECUTABLE CHANNEL AVAILABILITY

For context-enabled policies, the MCP tool channel is marked unavailable from hour 24
onward. Institutional retrieval is not automatically disabled by this scenario, and the
normal policy distribution continues to operate using the channels available to its
mode. The benchmark records channel availability and decisions for later validation.

3. EXCLUDED CLAIMS

The code does not implement heartbeat polling, a 48-hour cached-policy store, paper
outage logs, two-hour reconciliation, post-incident deadlines, or queued blockchain
transactions. It does not simulate a real cyberattack or validate operational recovery.
Those procedures require a separately designed and tested deployment plan.
