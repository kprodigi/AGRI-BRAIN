Source scope: Constructed institutional-retrieval input for the synthetic AGRI-BRAIN benchmark. It is not law, certification, field evidence, measured performance, or operational advice. Document ID: KB-RFB-002
Executable sources: agribrain/backend/src/models/policy.py, slca.py, waste.py, and reverse_logistics.py

local_redistribute is one of three canonical policy actions. Its nominal modeled route
distance is 45 km before the keyed stochastic transport multiplier. Its author-declared
social priors are L=0.82, R=0.78, and P=0.78. Its action-specific waste-saving coefficient
is 0.45, scaled by 1/(1+0.10*surplus_ratio). These are synthetic outcome coefficients,
not measured benefits.

The descriptive recovery-option module computes
food_bank_raw=max(0,1-2*rho), halved when temperature exceeds 10 degrees Celsius, then
normalizes it with animal-feed and composting heuristic scores. This is a circularity
proxy only. It does not certify safety or eligibility, and rho=0.30 is a policy logit
knee rather than a donation or consumption boundary.

The benchmark does not inspect food, accept or reject a lot, identify a receiving
organization, impose a transfer time, collect signatures, create transfer forms, or
verify traceability and cold-storage capability. Operational redistribution requires an
independent product-safety, recipient, logistics, and legal assessment.
