MODELED TRANSPORT-EMISSIONS INDICATOR
Status: Constructed benchmark input; the equation and coefficients are synthetic assumptions, not a fleet inventory.
Source scope: Constructed institutional-retrieval input for the synthetic AGRI-BRAIN benchmark. It is not law, certification, field evidence, measured performance, or operational advice. External sources, where named, motivate the activity-times-factor structure only; the executable code and locked protocol govern all benchmark values.
Document ID: KB-CAT-008
Executable sources: agribrain/backend/src/models/carbon.py and policy.py

1. EXECUTABLE EQUATION

For one standardized routing opportunity, the confirmatory simulator computes

  theta = clamp((T_C - 4.0) / 20.0, 0, 1)
  E = d * 0.12 * 1.0 * (1 + 0.40*theta) kilograms CO2-equivalent.

Here d is the selected route's nominal distance multiplied by the keyed stochastic
transport multiplier. Nominal distances are 120 km for cold_chain, 45 km for
local_redistribute, and 80 km for recovery. The confirmatory physical-efficiency factor
is 1.0 for every mode. Thermal stress rises linearly from zero at 4 degrees Celsius to
one at 24 degrees Celsius; the equation does not switch at 35 degrees Celsius.

The 0.12 kg CO2-equivalent per vehicle-kilometre factor and the 0.40 full-stress penalty
are author-declared benchmark coefficients. They are not measured fleet values and are
not attributed to an EPA factor table.

2. SOCIAL-PROXY CARBON COMPONENT

The simulator converts E to C = max(0, 1 - E/50), where 50 kg CO2-equivalent is a
normalizer for one standardized routing opportunity, not an episode emissions cap. The
social-performance proxy then uses

  S = 0.30*C + 0.20*L + 0.25*R + 0.25*P.

The episode ARI-per-modeled-emissions ratio is mean ARI divided by cumulative modeled
episode emissions; no factor of 1,000 is applied. This descriptive ratio is not a
measured carbon-efficiency or lifecycle-performance assessment.

3. EXCLUDED CLAIMS

The benchmark does not infer shipment payload, tonne-kilometres, fuel type, ownership,
Scope 1/2/3 category, monthly inventory, carbon budget, or regulatory reporting duty.
It does not require a blockchain record. Real emissions reporting requires traceable
vehicle-, load-, fuel-, refrigeration-, location-, and time-specific inventory data.
