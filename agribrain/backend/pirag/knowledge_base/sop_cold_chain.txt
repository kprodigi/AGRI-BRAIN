stochastic transport multiplier. Its modeled emissions use the same mode-neutral
activity equation as the other actions. Selecting cold_chain does not alter the common
latent spoilage trajectory or trigger an unimplemented physical control.

The benchmark does not perform harvesting, precooling, loading-dock control, packaging
inspection, microbial testing, vehicle refrigeration control, GPS tracking, live
telemetry, or equipment verification. Those activities require a separately validated
operating procedure and product-specific evidence.
