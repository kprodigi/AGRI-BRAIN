1. EXECUTABLE COMMUNITY-RESILIENCE PRIORS

The simulator uses one fixed community-resilience prior R for each canonical routing
action:

  - cold_chain: R = 0.55
  - local_redistribute: R = 0.78
  - recovery: R = 0.72

These values encode a synthetic ordering, local_redistribute greater than recovery
greater than cold_chain. They are not observations of food access, household welfare,
community capacity, or distributional equity. The benchmark does not load census-tract
data or a USDA Food Access Research Atlas overlay.

2. ROLE IN THE SOCIAL-PERFORMANCE PROXY

R receives the author-declared weight w_R = 0.25 in

  S = 0.30*C + 0.20*L + 0.25*R + 0.25*P.

C is a modeled transport-emissions term. L, R, and P are action-level scenario priors.
The resulting S is a social-performance proxy, not a Social Life-Cycle Assessment based
on a stakeholder or geographic inventory.

3. EXCLUDED CLAIMS

No delivery-volume, meal-count, household-income, economic-multiplier, census-tract, or
quarterly-report variable enters this score. Any deployment study would require an
independently sourced geographic and stakeholder inventory and a separately validated
assessment; those data and claims are outside this benchmark.
