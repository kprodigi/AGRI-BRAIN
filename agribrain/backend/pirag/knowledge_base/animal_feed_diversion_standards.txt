1. EXECUTABLE ACTION SCOPE

The decision policy has exactly three canonical actions: cold_chain,
local_redistribute, and recovery. Animal feed is not a separately selectable action; it
is one contextual subpath considered by the aggregate recovery proxy.

For non-hybrid rho-shaped policies, rho greater than 0.30 activates an author-declared
recovery logit knee. This 0.30 value is a synthetic policy hyperparameter, not a product
eligibility, marketability, microbial, or safety threshold.

2. EXECUTABLE RECOVERY-OPTION HEURISTIC

The descriptive recovery module computes three unvalidated suitability scores:

  food_bank_raw = max(0, 1 - 2*rho), then multiplied by 0.5 when T > 10 C;
  animal_feed_raw = min(1, 0.3 + 0.8*rho), then multiplied by 1.2 and capped
                    at 1 when inventory > 15000;
  composting_raw = min(1, 0.2 + rho).

The three scores are normalized to sum to one, and the largest is labelled the
recommended subpath. These are stylized calculations, not eligibility determinations.
The primary circular-economy proxy for the recovery action is
min(1, 0.5 + 0.5*max(normalized subpath score)).

3. EXCLUDED CLAIMS

The benchmark does not ingest microbial tests, packaging inspections, chemical tests,
species-specific feed rules, laboratory accreditation, certificates, destination feed
mills, retention records, prices, or recovery revenue. Any operational diversion to
animal feed requires an independent safety and legal assessment outside this benchmark.
