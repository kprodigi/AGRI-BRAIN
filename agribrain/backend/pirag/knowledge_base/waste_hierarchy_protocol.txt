circular-economy proxy returns 0 for cold_chain, 0.4+0.6*food_bank for
local_redistribute (capped at 1), and 0.5+0.5*the largest normalized score for recovery
(capped at 1). These are synthetic indicators, not measured circularity or disposition
decisions.
