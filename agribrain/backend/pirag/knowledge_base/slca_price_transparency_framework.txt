1. EXECUTABLE PRICE-TRANSPARENCY PRIORS

The simulator uses one fixed price-transparency prior P for each canonical routing
action:

  - cold_chain: P = 0.55
  - local_redistribute: P = 0.78
  - recovery: P = 0.68

These values encode a synthetic ordering, local_redistribute greater than recovery
greater than cold_chain. They were not calculated from prices, margins, invoices,
transaction records, subsidy records, donation valuations, or external audits.

2. ROLE IN THE SOCIAL-PERFORMANCE PROXY

P receives the author-declared weight w_P = 0.25 in

  S = 0.30*C + 0.20*L + 0.25*R + 0.25*P.

The route-level P prior is evaluated by the outcome equation for the selected action.
Institutional retrieval can supply context to a context-enabled policy, but retrieved
text does not replace the executable score table.

3. EXCLUDED CLAIMS

right, response deadline, quarterly price report, or measured consumer-information
outcome. A real transparency assessment would require independently collected and
