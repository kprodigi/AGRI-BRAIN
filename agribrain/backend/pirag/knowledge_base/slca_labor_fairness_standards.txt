1. EXECUTABLE LABOUR-FAIRNESS PRIORS

The simulator uses one fixed labour-fairness prior L for each canonical routing action:

  - cold_chain: L = 0.60
  - local_redistribute: L = 0.82
  - recovery: L = 0.70

These values encode a synthetic ordering, local_redistribute greater than recovery
greater than cold_chain. They are not estimates of working hours, wages, fatigue,
occupational risk, rest periods, or worker experience.

2. ROLE IN THE SOCIAL-PERFORMANCE PROXY

L receives the author-declared weight w_L = 0.20 in

  S = 0.30*C + 0.20*L + 0.25*R + 0.25*P.

The route-level L prior is evaluated by the outcome equation for the selected action.
No personnel record or labour observation is ingested by the benchmark.

3. EXCLUDED CLAIMS

The benchmark does not implement shift-duration rules, driver-hours checks, rest-break
checks, GPS verification, payroll verification, worker interviews, labour audits, or a
labour-compliance log. A deployment claim about labour fairness would require an audited
worker and stakeholder inventory outside this benchmark.
