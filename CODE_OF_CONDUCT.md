# Community code of conduct

AGRI-BRAIN contributors are expected to keep technical discussion rigorous,
respectful, and focused on improving the work.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive and specific when reviewing code, methods, or claims.
- Distinguish verified evidence from inference and opinion.
- Welcome questions from people with different backgrounds and experience.
- Credit contributions accurately and disclose relevant limitations.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing personal or confidential information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, deliberate
misrepresentation of evidence, and publication of another person's private
information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's Security reporting
channel. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation,
or take other proportionate action. Reports will be handled as confidentially
as practical, with attention to the safety of the reporter and affected
participants.
